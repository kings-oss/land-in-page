# Kings Voiceover

**“Your Story, Your Voice”**

This repository hosts the official landing page for **Kings Voiceover**, a professional voice-over service based in Nairobi, Kenya, serving clients locally and internationally.

## 🎙️ About
Kings Voiceover provides clear, expressive, and professional voice-over recordings for:
- Commercials (radio & digital)
- Corporate narrations
- E-learning and training content
- Podcasts and online media

All recordings are produced from a professional home studio with clean audio delivery.
